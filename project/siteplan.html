SimplTrak Business Tracker

Site Name: SimplTrak

This name represents a simple, web based form-driven tracker that helps small business owners keep up with inventory, orders and store sales without spreadsheets or complicated software.

Site Purpose:

SimplTrak gives small business owners one easy place to manage their business day to day. Users fill in simple forms to record inventory with barcode scanning, track wholesale orders, log sales at each store location and keep product recipes on file. The data is organized automatically, so there's no software to learn.

Scenarios:

  • How do I enter product information to be tracked?
  • How can I keep track of my product inventory at different store locations?
  • What orders have been made and have the orders been delivered?
